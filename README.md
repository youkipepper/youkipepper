## About / 简介

- 🎓 PhD student at Hohai University, working on structural health monitoring (SHM) and damage identification for transport structures
- 🌉 Focus: non-contact vision-based vibration / displacement measurement, cable force & modal identification, UAV-assisted bridge monitoring, event-camera sensing, and Abaqus–Blender simulation loops
- 📷 研究方向：交通结构健康监测与损伤识别；非接触视觉测振 / 测位移、索力与模态识别、无人机辅助桥梁监测、事件相机、以及 Abaqus–Blender 仿真闭环
- 🔗 ORCID: [0009-0008-8089-3449](https://orcid.org/0009-0008-8089-3449)

## Research Interests / 研究兴趣

- Vision-based vibrometry & modal analysis for bridges and cables
- Event-camera sensing for high-frequency / small-amplitude vibration
- UAV-based lightweight visual monitoring
- Simulation-in-the-loop validation (Abaqus × Blender synthetic video)
